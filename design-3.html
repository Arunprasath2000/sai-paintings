FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Is the site visit really free?

Yes. Gowtham or a senior painter visits, measures your space, shows shade cards and gives a fixed written quote — no charge, no obligation.

How long does a 2BHK take?

Usually 4–6 working days for interior painting, depending on wall condition and the finish you choose.

Do you move and cover furniture?

Yes. We cover furniture and floors, mask switches and fittings, and clean up every evening.

Which paint brands do you use?

We work with all major brands. We'll recommend the right grade for your budget, or use your preferred brand.

Do you give a warranty?

Yes — we offer a workmanship warranty on every job, plus the paint manufacturer's warranty on premium ranges.
